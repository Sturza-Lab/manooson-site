Core requirements

Everything below is essential for the live percussion + DJ set to sound and look the way it's designed to. Please confirm all points with your sound engineer ahead of the event.

014 microphones for percussion Shure Beta 57A / SM57 / Audix i5
022 microphones for cymbals AKG C451 / Rode NT5 or any condenser mic
036 medium boom stands One for each microphone
046 XLR cables One for each microphone
052 active stage monitors If two are not available, 1 monitor is acceptable
06Audio mixer with at least 6 available XLR inputs Position: the setup must be placed near the DJ booth
073 standard single-braced keyboard stands For drum mounting
082 standard cymbal stands Boom or straight
Critical point

A professional sound engineer must be on-site for the soundcheck and the performance to balance the percussion with the DJ set.

Instruments & logistics

What the artist brings, the stage he needs, and how the setup travels.

09Stage footprint: minimum 2m × 3m clear performative space, black or dark grey carpet/rug/mat surface For stand stability and noise isolation
Instruments brought by the artist
✦Darbuka (1×), Djembe (2×), Bongo (2×), Cymbals (2×)
✦Small percussion: tambourine, naff, maracas
✦Total instrument weight ~30 kg (excluding stands)
✦Extended percussion setup, additional drums/cymbals and LED drums available on request
Alternative: full local instrument rental

If the venue/promoter prefers to source the instrument setup locally to save on flight baggage costs, our technical team must contact the rental provider directly prior to confirmation to approve instrument specifications and quality.

Note: if any of the above cannot be provided, please inform us in advance.
