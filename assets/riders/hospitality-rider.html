Hospitality RiderMANOO SON

Travel & stay

Everything that keeps the artist rested, fed and on time for your event. Please confirm all points with the Organizer ahead of the booking.

01 · Accommodation & meals
✦Hotel: 4-star or 5-star accommodation provided and fully covered by the Organizer
✦Catering / daily allowance: full meal coverage or a daily catering allowance for the Artist on performance day(s)
02 · Ground transportation
✦Local logistics: the Organizer provides/covers all ground transfers — Airport ↔ Hotel ↔ Venue / Soundcheck / Show
03 · Air travel
✦Flights: all required round-trip airfare to and from the performance destination, arranged and fully covered by the Organizer
✦Baggage: the Organizer also covers checked baggage and excess/oversized baggage fees for instruments, percussion equipment and personal luggage

At the venue

What the venue needs to have ready before the artist arrives.

04 · Stage assistance & load-in
Critical point

Minimum 1–2 designated venue staff members required upon arrival to assist with loading, carrying heavy percussion equipment to the stage, and re-loading it back into the vehicle post-show.

05 · Dressing room (green room)
✦Private space: a clean, secure and lockable dressing room at the venue
✦Usage: storage for heavy flight cases/instrument cases, changing area, and artist rest zone between sets
✦Amenities: 3× bottles of still, room-temperature water and 3× clean, dry hand towels in the dressing room prior to the artist's arrival
06 · On-stage requirements
✦1× powerful personal stage fan, placed facing the artist's spot

Note: if any of the above cannot be provided, please inform us in advance.
