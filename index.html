Who is Manoo Son

Live rhythm for people who don't do average sets

MANOO SON is a professional percussionist and performance artist. On stage since 2014, working the format of live percussion support for DJs - adding rhythm, visuals, and dancefloor energy no track alone can carry.

Based in Goa, India. Touring India and Russia - Goa, Pune, Hyderabad, Manali, Lucknow, Moscow, Saint Petersburg.

"What I see while playing the drums" - it's the crowd, every time.
